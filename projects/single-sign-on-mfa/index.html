CASE-05

Single sign-on with MFA

One Keycloak login with one-time-password MFA, backed by Active Directory, for the security tools and business applications.

Status
In production
My role
Design and deployment
Where
AVEOLYS, Antananarivo
When
Since 2023
Tools
Keycloak, Active Directory, Kerberos, OIDC, SAML

The goal

Each application had its own accounts and passwords. More accounts mean more passwords to steal, and leavers whose access is forgotten somewhere.

What I deployed

  1. Active Directory
  2. →
  3. Keycloak + MFA
  4. →
  5. Applications
  • Keycloak as the identity provider, connected to Active Directory and Kerberos, so staff use their usual company account.
  • One-time-password MFA on top of the password.
  • Applications connected over OIDC or SAML, including Wazuh, Shuffle, Zimbra, Microsoft 365, Bitdefender GravityZone, KaseyaOne, the internal wiki and the internal ERP.

Result

Staff have one account to protect, and removing someone's access happens in one place.
