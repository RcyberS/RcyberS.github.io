Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-11-19.

Who is responsible

This website is published by Stephanio RAZARISON, Antananarivo, Madagascar. For any question about your data, email rbienvenustephanio@gmail.com.

Cookies

This site uses cookies for visit statistics with Google Analytics, never for advertising.

Preferences stored in your browser

If you switch the theme or the language, your choice is saved in your browser's local storage (the keys theme and lang) so the site remembers it. It never leaves your device. You can remove it by clearing your browser's site data.

Hosting

The site is hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA). Like any web host, GitHub processes technical data such as your IP address to deliver the pages and keep the service secure. See the GitHub privacy statement.

Visit statistics (Google Analytics)

I use Google Analytics 4 to count visits and see which pages are read, from which country and on which type of device. Google Analytics sets the cookies _ga and _ga_*, which last up to 2 years. Google does not record or store IP addresses in Google Analytics 4. Data is kept for 14 months, then deleted.

The data is processed by Google (Google Ireland Limited for visitors in Europe, Google LLC in the USA). Google LLC is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. See Google's privacy policy and how Google uses data from sites that use its services.

You can block these cookies in your browser settings, or install Google's opt-out add-on.

Contacting me

If you email me, I use your message only to reply to you, I don't share it, and I delete it within 24 months of our last exchange.

Links to other sites

Links to LinkedIn and Upwork open those services, which apply their own privacy policies.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal data I hold about you by emailing rbienvenustephanio@gmail.com. I'll reply within one month. If you are in the European Union, you can also contact your data protection authority.
